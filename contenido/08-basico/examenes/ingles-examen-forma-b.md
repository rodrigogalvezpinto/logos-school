# PRUEBA DE INGLÉS — 8° Básico — FORMA B
## Examen de Práctica estilo Examen Libre de Validación de Estudios (Mineduc)

**Asignatura:** Idioma Extranjero — Inglés
**Curso:** 8° Básico
**N° de preguntas:** 25
**Tiempo:** 90 minutos
**Materiales permitidos:** Se permite el uso de **diccionario** (impreso) durante toda
la prueba. No se permite el uso de calculadora, celular ni apuntes.

### Instrucciones para el estudiante

1. Lee cada pregunta y su texto antes de responder. No necesitas memorizar las
   respuestas de la guía: aquí encontrarás situaciones nuevas sobre los mismos temas.
2. En selección múltiple marca una alternativa (a, b, c o d). En las preguntas
   abiertas sigue la consigna: puede pedir una palabra, True/False o una oración.
3. Puedes usar diccionario para buscar palabras que no conozcas. El diccionario te
   ayuda con vocabulario puntual, pero no reemplaza el manejo de la gramática.
4. Cada pregunta vale un punto. No hay descuento por respuestas incorrectas.
5. En las respuestas personales se aceptan distintas ideas si cumplen el modelo
   y la función solicitada; no debes coincidir con el ejemplo de la pauta.
6. Administra tu tiempo: te recomendamos no más de 3 a 4 minutos por pregunta.

## OA N°9 — Comprender, resumir y cuestionar un texto

**1.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"In many cities, people can now rent
electric scooters with a mobile app. Users scan a code, ride to their
destination and leave the scooter on the street. Some neighbors like them, but
others complain that scooters block the sidewalks."* What is the text mainly
about?

a) Renting electric scooters with an app and people's opinions about them
b) How to build an electric scooter
c) Why mobile apps are expensive
d) The history of bicycles

**2.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar)* Read: *"Ana's class made a blog about
legends from Chiloé. Each student wrote a post and added a drawing. Now students
from other schools read the blog and write comments."* Complete the summary:
*"The text explains how Ana's class ___ a blog about legends from Chiloé, and
how other students ___ comments on it."* (created / write)

**3.** *(Pregunta abierta — verdadero o falso)* Read: *"The Atacama Desert is
one of the driest places on Earth. Its sky is usually very clear, so several
international observatories were built there."* Write True or False: *"The
Atacama Desert is very dry and has clear skies, so it has important
observatories" is a good one-sentence summary of the text.*

**4.** *(Pregunta abierta — responder en inglés)* Read: *"In Scotland, people
celebrate Hogmanay on 31st December with fireworks, music and a traditional
song called Auld Lang Syne."* Answer in English: *How do people in your town or
country celebrate New Year's Eve?*

**5.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Read: *"Some museums now offer
virtual tours, so people can visit them online from home."* Which question best
expands the information in the text (instead of just repeating it)?

a) Do some museums offer virtual tours?
b) Can people visit some museums online?
c) What is a museum?
d) How might virtual tours change the number of visitors in person?

**6.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar tabla)* Read: *"Hi, I'm Lucía. I'm
Peruvian and I live in Cusco. I'm 14 and my favorite app is a language-learning
app. I use it for twenty minutes every day."* Complete the table: Nationality:
___ / City: ___ / Time on the app every day: ___

## OA N°10 — Comprender textos no literarios: propósito, condición y formación de palabras

**7.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"How to make a video call: 1. Open the
app. 2. Choose a contact. 3. Press the camera button. 4. Wait for the other
person to answer."* What is the purpose of this text?

a) To persuade readers to buy a phone
b) To explain how to do something
c) To tell a funny story
d) To describe a famous person

**8.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar)* Complete with the correct connector:
*"You can play video games ___ dinner is ready. Then, turn off the console."*
(until / also)

**9.** *(Pregunta abierta — verdadero o falso)* Read: *"If you visit
Valparaíso, you will see colorful houses on the hills."* Write True or False:
*This sentence describes an action that already happened.*

**10.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar)* Complete with the comparative form:
*"Traveling by plane is ___ (fast) than traveling by bus."*

**11.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Read: *"I'm not sure about my
holidays. Maybe I will visit my cousins in Puerto Natales."* What does the word
*"maybe"* express in this text?

a) A consequence
b) Possibility
c) Addition
d) A time limit

**12.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar)* Complete with the noun formed from the
verb: *"The ___ (invent) of the printing press changed how people shared
information."* (usa la terminación *-ion*)

## OA N°13 — Escribir sobre experiencias, culturas y preferencias

**13.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar)* Complete with the base form of the verb:
*"I'd rather ___ (take) the train than the bus, because it is more
comfortable."*

**14.** *(Selección múltiple)* Which sentence best expresses a justified
preference about another culture?

a) Germany is a country in Europe.
b) Berlin has many museums.
c) I prefer Peru to Spain because I want to see Machu Picchu.
d) Many people in Spain speak Spanish.

**15.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar)* Complete about a trip: *"Last summer,
my family ___ (travel) to Chiloé by ferry. We ___ (try) curanto, a typical
dish."* (usa el pasado simple)

**16.** *(Pregunta abierta — verdadero o falso)* La oración *"Mexican food is
the most delicious food in the world"* es un hecho comprobable. Write True or
False.

**17.** *(Pregunta abierta — emparejar)* Relaciona la expresión con su
significado: *"I don't mind sharing my room"* → ¿"me molesta mucho compartir mi
pieza" o "no me importa compartir mi pieza"?

**18.** *(Pregunta abierta — escribir siguiendo modelo)* Read the model for a
travel poster: *"Landmark: the Eiffel Tower. Country: France. Why I want to go:
I want to see Paris from the top."* Write your own version about a landmark
you would like to visit: *"Landmark: ___. Country: ___. Why I want to go:
___."*

## OA N°16 — Usar estructuras gramaticales de futuro, comparación y preferencia al escribir

**19.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar)* Complete: *"Our team finished in ___
place. Only two teams were better than us."* (third / three)

**20.** *(Pregunta abierta — responder en inglés)* Read the poster: *"School
trip to La Serena. Monday 10th November. The bus leaves at 8:30."* Answer in
English: *When does the bus leave?*

**21.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar, desafiante)* Complete with will or going
to (decisión espontánea en el momento de hablar): *"— The phone is ringing! —
Don't worry, I ___ (answer) it."*

**22.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* ¿Qué oración describe correctamente
a una persona con la estructura *the woman with...*?

a) The woman with the red jacket is my aunt.
b) The woman with red jacket my aunt.
c) Woman the with red jacket is.
d) The woman is with.

**23.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar)* Complete with a frequency adverb: *"We
___ go to the cinema because the tickets are too expensive. We watch movies at
home."* (never / always)

**24.** *(Pregunta abierta — responder en inglés, desafiante)* Answer
justifying your preference: *"Would you prefer to live in a big city or in a
small town in the future? Why?"*

**25.** *(Pregunta abierta — completar, desafiante)* Complete with the correct
expressions: *"When the bus stopped, we ___ the bus and walked ___ the
museum."* (got off / into)
