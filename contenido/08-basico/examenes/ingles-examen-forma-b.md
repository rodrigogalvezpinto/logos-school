# PRUEBA DE INGLÉS — 8° Básico — FORMA B
## Examen de Práctica estilo Examen Libre de Validación de Estudios (Mineduc)

**Asignatura:** Idioma Extranjero — Inglés
**Curso:** 8° Básico
**N° de preguntas:** 25
**Tiempo:** 90 minutos
**Materiales permitidos:** Se permite el uso de **diccionario** (impreso) durante toda
la prueba. No se permite el uso de calculadora, celular ni apuntes.

### Instrucciones para el estudiante

1. Lee cada pregunta y su texto antes de responder. No necesitas memorizar las
   respuestas de la guía: aquí encontrarás situaciones nuevas sobre los mismos temas.
2. Marca una alternativa (a, b, c o d). Si una pregunta dice **«marca todas las correctas»**,
   puede haber más de una opción correcta y debes marcarlas todas. En las de verdadero o
   falso, marca True o False.
3. Puedes usar diccionario para buscar palabras que no conozcas. El diccionario te
   ayuda con vocabulario puntual, pero no reemplaza el manejo de la gramática.
4. Cada pregunta vale un punto. No hay descuento por respuestas incorrectas.
5. En las preguntas de opinión o de escritura según modelo, elige la opción que cumple el
   modelo y la función solicitada.
6. Administra tu tiempo: te recomendamos no más de 3 a 4 minutos por pregunta.

## OA N°9 — Comprender, resumir y cuestionar un texto

**1.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"In many cities, people can now rent
electric scooters with a mobile app. Users scan a code, ride to their
destination and leave the scooter on the street. Some neighbors like them, but
others complain that scooters block the sidewalks."* What is the text mainly
about?

a) Renting electric scooters with an app and people's opinions about them
b) How to build an electric scooter at home with simple tools
c) Why mobile apps are expensive for people in big cities
d) The history of bicycles and scooters in European cities

**2.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Read: *"Ana's class made a blog about legends from Chiloé. Each student wrote a post and added a drawing. Now students from other schools read the blog and write comments."* Complete the summary: *"The text explains how Ana's class ___ a blog about legends from Chiloé, and how other students ___ comments on it."*

a) created / write
b) creates / wrote
c) create / writing
d) created / reads

**3.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read: *"The Atacama Desert is
one of the driest places on Earth. Its sky is usually very clear, so several
international observatories were built there."* Write True or False: *"The
Atacama Desert is very dry and has clear skies, so it has important
observatories" is a good one-sentence summary of the text.*

**4.** *(Alternativas — conectar con el propio contexto)* Read: *"In Scotland, people celebrate Hogmanay on 31st December with fireworks, music and a traditional song called Auld Lang Syne."* *How do people in your town or country celebrate New Year's Eve?* Which answer connects the text with your own context?

a) In Scotland, people sing Auld Lang Syne on 31st December.
b) New Year is a traditional song with fireworks and music.
c) I am fourteen years old and I live with my family in Chile.
d) In my town, people celebrate with fireworks and a big family dinner.

**5.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Read: *"Some museums now offer
virtual tours, so people can visit them online from home."* Which question best
expands the information in the text (instead of just repeating it)?

a) Do some museums offer virtual tours for people at home?
b) Can people visit some museums online instead of in person?
c) What is a museum, and what kinds of objects does it have?
d) How might virtual tours change the number of visitors in person?

**6.** *(Alternativas — completar tabla)* Read: *"Hi, I'm Lucía. I'm Peruvian and I live in Cusco. I'm 14 and my favorite app is a language-learning app. I use it for twenty minutes every day."* Complete: Nationality: ___ / City: ___ / Time on the app every day: ___

a) Chilean / Cusco / fourteen minutes
b) Peruvian / Cusco / twenty minutes
c) Peruvian / Lima / twenty minutes
d) Cusco / Peruvian / 14

## OA N°10 — Comprender textos no literarios: propósito, condición y formación de palabras

**7.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"How to make a video call: 1. Open the
app. 2. Choose a contact. 3. Press the camera button. 4. Wait for the other
person to answer."* What is the purpose of this text?

a) To persuade readers to buy a phone
b) To explain how to do something
c) To tell a funny story
d) To describe a famous person

**8.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete with the correct connector: *"You can play video games ___ dinner is ready. Then, turn off the console."*

a) also
b) because
c) until
d) first

**9.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read: *"If you visit
Valparaíso, you will see colorful houses on the hills."* Write True or False:
*This sentence describes an action that already happened.*

**10.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"Traveling by plane is ___ than traveling by bus."* (fast, comparative)

a) faster
b) more fast
c) fastest
d) the fastest

**11.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Read: *"I'm not sure about my
holidays. Maybe I will visit my cousins in Puerto Natales."* What does the word
*"maybe"* express in this text?

a) A consequence
b) Possibility
c) Addition
d) A time limit

**12.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete with the noun formed from the verb *invent*: *"The ___ of the printing press changed how people shared information."*

a) inventer
b) inventing
c) invented
d) invention

## OA N°13 — Escribir sobre experiencias, culturas y preferencias

**13.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"I'd rather ___ the train than the bus, because it is more comfortable."*

a) to take
b) take
c) taking
d) took

**14.** *(Selección múltiple)* Which sentence best expresses a justified
preference about another culture?

a) Germany is a country in Europe with many old cities.
b) Berlin has many museums, parks and famous buildings.
c) I prefer Peru to Spain because I want to see Machu Picchu.
d) Many people in Spain speak Spanish, like people in Chile.

**15.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete about a trip: *"Last summer, my family ___ to Chiloé by ferry. We ___ curanto, a typical dish."* (travel / try, simple past)

a) travel / try
b) traveled / tryed
c) traveled / tried
d) travels / tries

**16.** *(Verdadero o falso)* La oración *"Mexican food is
the most delicious food in the world"* es un hecho comprobable. Write True or
False.

**17.** *(Alternativas — vocabulario)* What does *"I don't mind sharing my room"* mean?

a) No me importa compartir mi pieza
b) Me molesta mucho compartir mi pieza
c) No quiero compartir mi pieza
d) Comparto mi pieza todos los días

**18.** *(Alternativas — escribir siguiendo modelo)* Model: *"Landmark: the Eiffel Tower. Country: France. Why I want to go: I want to see Paris from the top."* Which option follows the model correctly?

a) Landmark: Machu Picchu. Country: Mexico. Why I want to go: I want to learn about Inca history.
b) Landmark: Peru. Country: Machu Picchu. Why I want to go: history.
c) Landmark: Machu Picchu. Country: Peru. Why I want to go: yesterday.
d) Landmark: Machu Picchu. Country: Peru. Why I want to go: I want to learn about Inca history.

## OA N°16 — Usar estructuras gramaticales de futuro, comparación y preferencia al escribir

**19.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"Our team finished in ___ place. Only two teams were better than us."*

a) three
b) third
c) second
d) the three

**20.** *(Alternativas — comprensión)* Read the poster: *"School trip to La Serena. Monday 10th November. The bus leaves at 8:30."* *When does the bus leave?*

a) It leaves on Sunday.
b) It leaves in La Serena.
c) It leaves at 8:30.
d) It leaves at 10:00.

**21.** *(Alternativas — completar, desafiante)* Complete (spontaneous decision): *"— The phone is ringing! — Don't worry, I ___ it."*

a) will answer
b) am going to answer
c) answered
d) answering

**22.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* ¿Qué oración describe correctamente
a una persona con la estructura *the woman with...*?

a) The woman with the red jacket is my aunt.
b) The woman with red jacket my aunt is.
c) Woman the with red jacket is my aunt.
d) The woman is with the red jacket aunt.

**23.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"We ___ go to the cinema because the tickets are too expensive. We watch movies at home."*

a) always
b) usually
c) often
d) never

**24.** *(Varias correctas — preferencia, desafiante)* *"Would you prefer to live in a big city or in a small town in the future? Why?"* Which answers justify the preference correctly? Marca **todas** las correctas.

a) I would prefer to live in a small town because it is quiet and safe.
b) I would prefer to live in a big city because there are more opportunities to study.
c) I would prefer a big city because yesterday.
d) Small town big city.

**25.** *(Alternativas — completar, desafiante)* Complete: *"When the bus stopped, we ___ the bus and walked ___ the museum."*

a) got on / into
b) got off / into
c) got off / on
d) get off / at
