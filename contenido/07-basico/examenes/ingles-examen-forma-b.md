# PRUEBA DE INGLÉS — 7° Básico — FORMA B
## Examen de Práctica estilo Examen Libre de Validación de Estudios (Mineduc)

**Asignatura:** Idioma Extranjero — Inglés
**Curso:** 7° Básico
**N° de preguntas:** 25
**Tiempo:** 90 minutos
**Materiales permitidos:** Se permite el uso de **diccionario** (impreso) durante toda
la prueba. No se permite el uso de calculadora, celular ni apuntes.

### Instrucciones para el estudiante

1. Lee cada pregunta y su texto antes de responder. No necesitas memorizar las
   respuestas de la guía: aquí encontrarás situaciones nuevas sobre los mismos temas.
2. Marca una alternativa (a, b, c o d). Si una pregunta dice **«marca todas las correctas»**,
   puede haber más de una opción correcta y debes marcarlas todas. En las de verdadero o
   falso, marca True o False.
3. Puedes usar diccionario para buscar palabras que no conozcas. El diccionario te
   ayuda con vocabulario puntual, pero no reemplaza el manejo de la gramática.
4. Cada pregunta vale un punto. No hay descuento por respuestas incorrectas.
5. En las preguntas de opinión o de escritura según modelo, elige la opción que cumple el
   modelo y la función solicitada.
6. Administra tu tiempo: te recomendamos no más de 3 a 4 minutos por pregunta.

## OA N°9 — Comprender la idea general y la información explícita de un texto

**1.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Last Saturday, the students of a small
school in Iquique cleaned their local beach. They collected bottles, plastic
bags and cans. After that, they separated everything for recycling."* What is
the text mainly about?

a) A sports competition on the beach
b) A new school in Iquique
c) How to swim safely
d) Students cleaning a beach

**2.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Hi! I'm Kenji. I live in Osaka, Japan,
but my mother is Chilean. I started doing karate when I was six. Now I'm
thirteen and I train four times a week."* According to the text, how often
does Kenji train?

a) Six times a week
b) Four times a week
c) Thirteen times a week
d) Once a week

**3.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Read: *"Rapa Nui is an island in the Pacific Ocean. It belongs to Chile and it is famous for its moai, big statues made of stone."* Complete with one word from the text: *"The moai are big statues made of ___."*

a) stone
b) wood
c) island
d) ocean

**4.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read: *"Our school garden has
tomatoes, lettuce and carrots. The students water the plants every morning
before class."* Write True or False: *The students water the plants after
school.*

**5.** *(Alternativas — función)* ¿Qué función cumple la oración *"According to the text, the festival starts in March."*?

a) Expresar una postura personal del lector
b) Hacer una pregunta sobre el festival
c) Dar una instrucción para ir al festival
d) Dar información explícita del texto

**6.** *(Varias correctas — opinión, desafiante)* Read: *"Some families in our town want to close the main street to cars every Sunday, so people can walk and ride bikes there."* Which completions of *"I ___ with this idea because ___."* express a position with a coherent reason? Marca **todas** las correctas.

a) I agree with this idea because people can walk safely and the air is cleaner.
b) I disagree with this idea because some shops need cars to bring their products.
c) I agree with this idea because Sunday is a color.
d) I disagree with this idea because bikes are made of chocolate.

## OA N°10 — Comprender textos no literarios: secuencia, conectores y causa-efecto

**7.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Lionel Messi was born in Rosario,
Argentina, in 1987. When he was a child, he played for a small local team.
Later, he moved to Spain and played for FC Barcelona."* What type of text is
this?

a) A short biography
b) A recipe
c) An advertisement
d) A set of instructions

**8.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete with the correct connector: *"Put on your swimming cap ___ you get into the pool."*

a) after
b) before
c) while
d) finally

**9.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read: *"Chess is a board game
for two players. I think it is the most relaxing hobby."* Write True or False:
*The second sentence is an opinion.*

**10.** *(Alternativas — vocabulario)* What does *"unhealthy"* mean?

a) saludable
b) muy saludable
c) poco saludable
d) salud

**11.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"First, the players shook hands. After
that, the referee started the match."* What relationship do the connectors
*First* and *After that* express?

a) Cause and effect
b) Opinion
c) Sequence
d) Comparison

**12.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"Ride your bike ___ on the bike path."* (safe + -ly)

a) safely
b) safe
c) safety
d) safer

## OA N°13 — Escribir historias e información con apoyo de imágenes

**13.** *(Selección múltiple)* Which connector best completes this story? *"Last
winter, I tried snowboarding for the first time. First, I fell down many
times. ___, I could go down the hill without falling."*

a) Before
b) Finally
c) Because
d) Too

**14.** *(Selección múltiple)* You are writing about the culture of another
country. Which sentence shares cultural information?

a) Mexican families decorate altars with flowers for the Day of the Dead.
b) I am very tired today because I studied a lot for my test.
c) My favorite color is blue, and I like to wear blue clothes.
d) You mustn't run in the corridor because you can fall down.

**15.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete about a personal experience: *"Yesterday, my team ___ the final match, and we ___ very proud."* (win / feel, simple past)

a) winned / feeled
b) win / feel
c) was winning / feeling
d) won / felt

**16.** *(Alternativas — completar siguiendo modelo)* Which option completes the model *"My neighbours are ___. I think this helps our community because ___."* correctly?

a) clean the playground / children can play safely
b) cleaning the playground / children can play safely
c) cleaning the playground / I like pizza
d) happy / the playground

**17.** *(Alternativas — escribir siguiendo modelo)* Model: *"My favorite hobby is collecting stamps. I do it every weekend with my grandfather."* Which option follows the model *"My favorite hobby is ___. I do it ___ with ___."* correctly?

a) play board games / on Fridays / my cousins
b) playing board games / my cousins / on Fridays
c) playing board games / on Fridays / my cousins
d) on Fridays / playing board games / my cousins

**18.** *(Varias correctas — consejos, desafiante)* You are writing a poster about earthquakes. Which sentences with *must* or *mustn't* give safe advice for an earthquake? Marca **todas** las correctas.

a) During an earthquake, you must stay away from windows.
b) You mustn't use the elevator.
c) You must run into the elevator.
d) You mustn't protect your head.

## OA N°16 — Usar estructuras gramaticales variadas al escribir

**19.** *(Alternativas — vocabulario)* What does *"I'm afraid of spiders"* mean?

a) Tengo miedo de las arañas
b) Me gustan mucho las arañas
c) Estoy buscando arañas
d) Tengo una araña

**20.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"The choir sang ___ at the school concert."* (beautiful, adverb of manner)

a) beautiful
b) beauty
c) more beautiful
d) beautifully

**21.** *(Selección múltiple)* Which sentence correctly expresses cause and
effect?

a) Plants are green and grow in many places.
b) My grandmother has many plants in her garden.
c) Plants dry up if you don't water them.
d) This plant is in the kitchen, near the window.

**22.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete with past continuous and past simple: *"While we ___ volleyball on the beach, a big wave ___ our ball."* (play / take)

a) played / was taking
b) were playing / took
c) are playing / takes
d) was playing / taked

**23.** *(Varias correctas — respuesta corta)* *"— Did you watch the match last night? — ___"* Which short answers are correct? Marca **todas** las correctas.

a) Yes, I did.
b) No, I didn't.
c) Yes, I watched.
d) No, I don't.

**24.** *(Selección múltiple)* Complete: *"Look at ___ birds over there, near
the lake!"*

a) this
b) that
c) these
d) those

**25.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"How ___ water do you drink every day?"*

a) many
b) few
c) much
d) a
