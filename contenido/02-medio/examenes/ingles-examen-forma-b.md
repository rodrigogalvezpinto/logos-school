# EXAMEN DE INGLÉS — 2° Medio — FORMA B
## Examen de Práctica estilo Examen Libre de Validación de Estudios (Mineduc)

**Asignatura:** Idioma Extranjero — Inglés
**Curso:** 2° Medio
**N° de preguntas:** 25
**Tiempo:** 90 minutos
**Materiales permitidos:** Se permite el uso de **diccionario** (impreso) durante toda
la prueba. No se permite el uso de calculadora, celular ni apuntes.

### Instrucciones para el estudiante

1. Lee cada pregunta con atención. Algunas preguntas incluyen un texto corto en
   inglés (estímulo) que debes leer antes de responder.
2. Marca con una X la alternativa correcta (a, b, c o d) o, en las de verdadero o falso, True o
   False. Si una pregunta dice **«marca todas las correctas»**, puede haber más de una opción
   correcta y debes marcarlas todas. La retroalimentación se entrega en español para que
   revises tu razonamiento.
3. Puedes usar diccionario para buscar palabras que no conozcas. El diccionario te
   ayuda con vocabulario puntual, pero no reemplaza el manejo de la gramática ni la
   fluidez con las estructuras del año — no vas a tener tiempo de buscar cada palabra.
4. Todas las preguntas valen el mismo puntaje. No hay descuento por respuestas
   incorrectas: si no estás seguro/a, responde de todas formas.
5. Administra tu tiempo: te recomendamos no más de 3 a 4 minutos por pregunta.

## OA N°9 — Comprender ideas generales e información explícita en textos variados

**1.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"3D printing technology allows people to
create objects layer by layer using digital designs. Hospitals now use it to
produce affordable prosthetic limbs for patients around the world."*

What information is explicit in the text?

a) 3D printing allows hospitals to produce affordable prosthetic limbs.
b) 3D printing is only used to make toys and decorations.
c) 3D printing cannot be used to create physical objects at all.
d) 3D printing has never been used in medicine or in hospitals.

**2.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"This text is ___ how coral reefs are dying because of rising ocean temperatures. It explains ___ scientists are trying to grow new coral in laboratories."*

a) about / how
b) how / about
c) of / what
d) about / who

**3.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read: *"Wind turbines
produce clean electricity and, according to recent studies, are far quieter
than most people assume when installed at a safe distance from homes."*
Statement: *According to the text, wind turbines are known to be extremely
noisy no matter where they are installed.*

**4.** *(Alternativas — comprensión)* Read: *"The first website was published in 1990, and by the mid-1990s the internet had begun reaching homes worldwide."* *When was the first website published?*

a) It was published in the mid-1990s.
b) It was published in 1909.
c) It was published worldwide.
d) It was published in 1990.

**5.** *(Alternativas — completar esquema)* Read: *"Jane Goodall is a British primatologist. She was born in 1934 and became famous for her decades of research on chimpanzee behavior in Tanzania."* Nationality: ___ / Year of birth: ___ / Achievement: ___

a) Tanzanian / 1934 / became famous for her research on chimpanzees
b) British / 1934 / became famous for her research on chimpanzee behavior
c) British / 1943 / won a Nobel Prize
d) Primatologist / British / 1934

**6.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Read an article about artificial
intelligence that includes unfamiliar vocabulary (*algorithms, neural
networks*). What is the most useful strategy to understand the text without
translating every word?

a) Stop at every unknown word and look it up in the dictionary immediately.
b) Ignore the whole text if a difficult word appears in the first paragraph.
c) Use context and known words to infer the general meaning before using a dictionary.
d) Memorize the text word by word without trying to understand its meaning.

## OA N°10 — Comprender textos no literarios: propósito, relaciones de importancia, problema-solución y formación de palabras

**7.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Try public transport! It is cheaper
than driving and reduces pollution — leave your car at home twice a week."*
What is the purpose of this text?

a) To entertain readers with a funny story about cars
b) To sell tickets for a new bus company
c) To describe a person's daily routine in the city
d) To persuade/instruct readers to change a behavior

**8.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"___ schools teach digital skills early, students will not be ready for the jobs of the future."*

a) As a result
b) Because
c) Unless
d) So

**9.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read: *"Plastic bottles
were piling up at the school every week, so the principal installed water
refill stations across the campus."* Statement: *This sentence shows a
problem-solution relationship, and as a reader you can think of other
possible solutions not mentioned in the text, like banning plastic bottles
completely.*

**10.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"The map was ___ and several hikers got lost following it."* (correct + in-)

a) incorrect
b) uncorrect
c) imcorrect
d) correct

**11.** *(Alternativas — vocabulario)* What is the antonym of *"legible"*?

a) inlegible
b) unlegible
c) dislegible
d) illegible

**12.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Read: *"Neither the school nor
the parents' association attended the emergency meeting about the schedule
change."* ¿Qué indica esta oración?

a) Que ninguno de los dos actores asistió a la reunión.
b) Que ambos actores asistieron juntos a la reunión.
c) Que solo la escuela asistió a la reunión de emergencia.
d) Que no hubo ninguna reunión sobre el cambio de horario.

## OA N°13 — Escribir sobre experiencias, temas interdisciplinarios, problemas globales y culturas

**13.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"___ my opinion, every neighborhood should have a recycling program."*

a) At
b) In
c) On
d) By

**14.** *(Selección múltiple)* ¿Qué oración expresa una opinión justificada sobre la contaminación de los océanos?

a) The ocean is very big and has many fish.
b) My family goes to the beach every summer.
c) I think we should ban single-use plastics, because they end up in the ocean and harm marine life.
d) There are many beaches in the north of Chile.

**15.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"Last month, in Science class, we ___ a documentary about the oceans. I ___ a lot about marine life."* (watch / learn, simple past)

a) watch / learn
b) watched / learnt to
c) watched / learned
d) watching / learned

**16.** *(Verdadero o falso)* La oración *"For example,
in Sweden many people value a balance between work and free time, often
leaving the office early to spend time with family"* es un buen recurso
para ilustrar una idea sobre otra cultura.

**17.** *(Alternativas — escribir siguiendo modelo)* Which option completes *"One problem that affects my city is ___. I'd rather ___ than ___ because ___."* coherently?

a) air pollution / ride my bike / go by car / it reduces emissions
b) air pollution / go by car / ride my bike / it reduces emissions
c) my bike / air pollution / car / because
d) air pollution / ride my bike / go by car / I like pizza

**18.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Estás escribiendo sobre un texto
que leíste acerca de un joven ingeniero. ¿Qué oración resume mejor la
información respetando la finalidad del OA?

a) An engineer. Prosthetic hands. Children. Since 2019. The text.
b) The text I read was about a teenage engineer who has been designing
   affordable prosthetic hands for children since 2019.
c) Hands are useful, and engineers design many things that people use every day.
d) I don't remember the text very well, but I think it was about a young person who likes science and builds robots at home.

## OA N°16 — Usar estructuras gramaticales de presente perfecto continuo, pasado perfecto, estilo indirecto, condicionales y conectores avanzados al escribir

**19.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"There are ___ opportunities to study abroad in this program, so competition is high."*

a) little
b) much
c) a little
d) few

**20.** *(Selección múltiple)* Completa la oración: "Tomás is tired because he ______ for the exam since seven o'clock."

a) studies
b) has been studying
c) studied
d) is study

**21.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete with the second conditional: *"Our school does not have a garden. If our school ___ a garden, we would grow vegetables."*

a) has
b) had
c) will have
d) would have

**22.** *(Verdadero o falso)* La oración *"The concert
had already started when the band's singer finally arrived"* describe una
acción que ocurrió **antes** de otra acción pasada.

**23.** *(Alternativas — frecuencia)* *"How often does the school publish its online newsletter?"* Which answer is correct?

a) Last month.
b) For a month.
c) Once a month.
d) A month ago.

**24.** *(Alternativas — estilo indirecto)* My cousin said: *"I have visited three countries this year."* → *My cousin said he ___ three countries that year.*

a) had visited
b) has visited
c) visits
d) will visit

**25.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Completa la oración: "The children made the posters ______; no adult helped them."

a) them
b) themselves
c) their
d) theirs
