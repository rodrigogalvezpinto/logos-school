# PRUEBA DE INGLÉS — 1° Medio — FORMA B
## Examen de Práctica estilo Examen Libre de Validación de Estudios (Mineduc)

**Asignatura:** Idioma Extranjero — Inglés
**Curso:** 1° Medio
**N° de preguntas:** 25
**Tiempo:** 90 minutos
**Materiales permitidos:** Se permite el uso de **diccionario** (impreso) durante toda
la prueba. No se permite el uso de calculadora, celular ni apuntes.

### Instrucciones para el estudiante

1. Lee cada pregunta con atención. Algunas preguntas incluyen un texto corto en
   inglés (estímulo) que debes leer antes de responder.
2. Marca con una X la alternativa correcta (a, b, c o d) o, en las de verdadero o falso, True o
   False. Si una pregunta dice **«marca todas las correctas»**, puede haber más de una opción
   correcta y debes marcarlas todas. La retroalimentación se entrega en español para que
   revises tu razonamiento.
3. Puedes usar diccionario para buscar palabras que no conozcas. El diccionario te
   ayuda con vocabulario puntual, pero no reemplaza el manejo de la gramática ni la
   fluidez con el vocabulario frecuente — no vas a tener tiempo de buscar cada palabra.
4. Todas las preguntas valen el mismo puntaje. No hay descuento por respuestas
   incorrectas: si no estás seguro/a, responde de todas formas.
5. Administra tu tiempo: te recomendamos no más de 3 a 4 minutos por pregunta.
6. Esta es la **Forma B** de la prueba: cubre los mismos 4 Objetivos de Aprendizaje
   que el banco de contenido, pero con preguntas y textos distintos.

## OA N°9 — Comprender ideas generales e información explícita en textos variados

**1.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Rosalind Franklin was an English
scientist whose X-ray images were essential to discovering the structure of
DNA. She died before receiving the recognition she deserved."* What
information is explicit in the text?

a) Rosalind Franklin's X-ray images were essential to the discovery of the structure of DNA.
b) Rosalind Franklin discovered the structure of DNA completely on her own.
c) Rosalind Franklin worked only in mathematics and never used X-ray images.
d) Rosalind Franklin received a Nobel Prize for her work during her lifetime.

**2.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"This text is ___ the history of tea. It explains ___ this drink became popular around the world."*

a) about / how
b) how / about
c) of / what
d) to / who

**3.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read: *"Penguins spend most
of their lives at sea hunting for fish, but they return to the same
coastline every year to raise their chicks."* Statement: *According to the
text, penguins raise their chicks in the open ocean.*

**4.** *(Alternativas — comprensión)* Read: *"The first FIFA World Cup was held in Uruguay in 1930, with teams from thirteen countries taking part."* *When and where was the first FIFA World Cup held?*

a) It was held in 1913, in Uruguay.
b) It was held in 1930, in thirteen countries.
c) It was held in Brazil in 1930.
d) It was held in 1930, in Uruguay.

**5.** *(Alternativas — completar esquema)* Read: *"Gabriela Mistral was a Chilean poet. She was born in 1889 and won the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1945."* Nationality: ___ / Year of birth: ___ / Award: ___

a) Chilean / 1945 / Nobel Prize in Literature
b) Chilean / 1889 / Nobel Prize in Literature
c) Poet / 1889 / Chilean
d) Spanish / 1889 / Nobel Prize in Peace

**6.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Read a text about deep-sea
creatures that includes unfamiliar vocabulary (*bioluminescence, pressure*).
What is the most useful strategy to understand the text without translating
every word?

a) Stop at every unknown word and look it up in the dictionary immediately.
b) Ignore the whole text if a difficult word appears in the first paragraph.
c) Use context and known words to infer the general meaning before using a dictionary.
d) Memorize the text word by word without trying to understand its meaning.

## OA N°10 — Comprender textos no literarios: propósito, contraste, problema-solución y formación de palabras

**7.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Recycle today! Separate your paper,
glass and plastic before throwing them away."* What is the purpose of this
text?

a) To entertain readers with a funny story about trash
b) To sell a new recycling product
c) To describe a famous person's life
d) To persuade/instruct readers to change a behavior

**8.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"The store was closed when we arrived. ___, we found another one nearby that had everything we needed."*

a) So that
b) Because
c) However
d) Although

**9.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read: *"Homework was
overwhelming many students, so the teacher reduced the amount of work she
assigned each week."* Statement: *This sentence shows a problem-solution
relationship.*

**10.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete with the antonym: *"Her attendance was very ___: some weeks she came every day, and other weeks she missed almost every class."* (regular + ir-)

a) irregular
b) unregular
c) disregular
d) regular

**11.** *(Alternativas — vocabulario)* What is the occupation noun formed from *"guitar"*?

a) guitarrer
b) guitarman
c) guitary
d) guitarist

**12.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Read: *"The museum was both
educational and entertaining for the students."* ¿Qué indica la estructura
*"both...and"* en esta oración?

a) Que el museo fue las dos cosas al mismo tiempo: educativo y entretenido.
b) Que el museo no fue ni educativo ni entretenido para los estudiantes.
c) Que el museo solo fue entretenido, pero no educativo para el curso.
d) Que los estudiantes no visitaron el museo, sino que leyeron sobre él.

## OA N°13 — Escribir sobre experiencias, temas interdisciplinarios, problemas globales y culturas

**13.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"___ my opinion, climate change is one of the most urgent problems that requires global action today."*

a) At
b) In
c) On
d) By

**14.** *(Selección múltiple)* ¿Qué oración expresa una opinión justificada sobre la escasez de agua?

a) Water is a liquid that we drink every day.
b) My house has two bathrooms and a kitchen.
c) I believe we must save water, because many regions are suffering long droughts.
d) Rivers flow from the mountains to the sea.

**15.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"Last Friday, in English class, we ___ a short story from Canada. I ___ its surprising ending."* (read / like, simple past)

a) readed / liked
b) read / like
c) read / liked
d) reads / liked

**16.** *(Verdadero o falso)* La oración *"For example, in
Mexico people celebrate the Day of the Dead with colorful altars and
flowers"* es un buen recurso para ilustrar una idea sobre otra cultura.

**17.** *(Alternativas — escribir siguiendo modelo)* Which option completes the model *"I believe ___ is an important global problem. For example, ___."* coherently?

a) water pollution / many rivers near cities are full of waste
b) water pollution / I like swimming in the sea in summer
c) for example / water pollution is in many rivers
d) rivers near cities / important global problem

**18.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* Estás escribiendo sobre un texto
que leíste acerca de un músico famoso. ¿Qué oración resume mejor la
información respetando la finalidad del OA?

a) Beethoven was a composer. Music. Hearing. The text.
b) The text I read was about Beethoven, a composer who kept writing music
   even after he lost his hearing.
c) Music is beautiful, and I listen to classical music every day after school.
d) I don't remember the text very well, but I think it was about a famous composer who played the piano.

## OA N°16 — Usar estructuras gramaticales de presente perfecto, hábitos pasados, cualidades y oraciones subordinadas al escribir

**19.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"The stadium can hold ___ spectators for the final match."*

a) four thousands
b) four thousands of
c) four of thousand
d) four thousand

**20.** *(Selección múltiple)* Completa la oración: "We ______ in Valparaíso for five years, and we still love it."

a) live
b) have lived
c) lived
d) are live

**21.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete with the past simple passive: *"This mural ___ by our Art class last Friday."* (paint)

a) painted
b) was painted
c) is painted
d) was paint

**22.** *(Verdadero o falso)* La oración *"My family used
to live in a small town, but now we live in the capital"* describe un hábito
pasado que ya no ocurre.

**23.** *(Alternativas — duración)* *"How long has she been a pilot?"* Which answer is correct?

a) Ten years ago.
b) In ten years.
c) For ten years.
d) Since ten years.

**24.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete: *"The inventor ___ designed this solar lamp won a prize. The workshop, ___ neighbours repair broken furniture together, is open on Tuesdays."*

a) who / where
b) which / who
c) where / which
d) who / which

**25.** *(Selección múltiple — desafiante)* ¿Qué oración describe
correctamente una ocupación y una cualidad de la persona, usando
correctamente el presente perfecto?

a) She tourist guide ten years, she really cheerful with the visitors.
b) She has worked as a tourist guide for ten years and she is really cheerful.
c) Tourist guide cheerful ten years, she is working in the city.
d) She guides tourists, and she was cheerful when she worked there.
