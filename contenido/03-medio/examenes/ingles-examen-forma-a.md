# PRUEBA DE INGLÉS — 3° Medio (Forma A)
## Examen de práctica estilo Examen Libre de Validación de Estudios (Mineduc)

**N° de preguntas:** 25 · **Tiempo:** 90 minutos · **Puntaje total:** 25 puntos.
**Materiales permitidos:** diccionario impreso. Sin celular, apuntes ni calculadora.

### Instrucciones

- Lee los estímulos antes de responder. Son situaciones ficticias creadas para practicar; no representan a todas las personas de un país.
- Las **25 preguntas son de alternativas**. Marca una sola letra en cada una.
- Cada pregunta vale **1 punto**, sin descuento por error. En las preguntas de opinión o de escritura, elige la opción que cumple la consigna; no se evalúa una opinión determinada.
- Distribuye unos 25 minutos en OA1, 25 en OA3 y 40 en OA4 y revisión.

## OA N°1 — Comprender, organizar y comparar información central sobre otras culturas

*(Lee el o los textos y responde las preguntas 1 a 8.)*

**Texts A and B — Sharing food**

**A — A neighbourhood blog in Berlin, Germany**
> Our community fridge stands outside a bakery. Anyone can leave food that is still good, and anyone can take it, no questions asked. Volunteers check the dates every evening and remove anything that has expired. The fridge started after a shop owner noticed how much bread was thrown away. Today, the volunteers say that the fridge is less about free food and more about trust between strangers.

**B — A school bulletin in Valparaíso, Chile**
> Every Friday, our class cooks a community pot with vegetables donated by market stallholders. Neighbours bring their own containers and take a portion home. We do not write down names, because nobody should feel watched when asking for food. Unlike the fridge in Berlin, we cook the food together, so students also learn to plan a menu. Still, both projects use food that would otherwise be wasted.

**1.** *(Alternativas — organizador)* Which option completes the organizer correctly? Berlin / Main activity / What volunteers do — Valparaíso / Main activity / What students learn

a) Berlin: people cook together / sell bread — Valparaíso: a fridge / repair containers
b) Berlin: a bakery sale / bake bread — Valparaíso: a fridge / check dates
c) Berlin: a community fridge / check dates every evening — Valparaíso: a community pot / plan a menu
d) Berlin: a community pot / plan a menu — Valparaíso: a fridge / check dates

## OA N°3 — Analizar textos y construir una postura personal crítica

*(Lee el o los textos y responde las preguntas 9 a 16.)*

**Texts C and D — Phones in the library?**

**C — Student newspaper editorial**
> Many students research their homework on their phones, but phones are banned in the school library. We propose allowing phones for study purposes during the afternoon. Students would keep them on silent and use headphones. We are not asking for permission to play games or watch videos: responsible use is the point.

**D — Librarian's response**
> I agree that phones can be useful research tools. However, the library is the only quiet place for students who need to concentrate. Allowing phones everywhere might increase noise. I suggest reserving two tables near the entrance for phone research during one month, and then asking users whether the rest of the room stayed quiet. A trial might show us problems we cannot predict.

**9.** *(Alternativas — análisis)* In Text C, what does *"responsible use"* refer to?

a) Playing games quietly in the library during the afternoon
b) Watching videos with the volume very low at every table
c) Using phones freely in every room of the school building
d) Using phones only for study, on silent and with headphones

---

**10.** *(Alternativas — resumen guiado)* Which summary of Text C follows the model *proposal / reason / what is NOT requested*?

a) Students want to close the library completely, because nobody uses it for research anymore.
b) Students propose allowing phones for study, because they research on them, but they are not asking to play games.
c) Students propose banning phones everywhere in the school, because they make too much noise.
d) Students want new computers in the library, because phones are too small for homework.

---

**11.** *(Alternativas — comparación)* Which option states one point of agreement and one difference between C and D?

a) Both want to ban phones in the whole school; they disagree only about the price of headphones.
b) Both want a new and bigger library; they disagree only about its opening hours on Fridays.
c) Both see phones as useful for research; D worries more about noise for students who need quiet.
d) Both agree to allow phones everywhere at once; they disagree only about which games are allowed.

---

**12.** *(Varias correctas — postura crítica)* Should the library allow phones everywhere at once or try the two reserved tables first? Which answers give a position with a reason grounded in the texts? Marca **todas** las correctas.

*(Varias correctas: marca todas las alternativas correctas.)*

a) Try the two tables first, because the library is the only quiet place and a trial can show problems.
b) Allow phones everywhere, because I like my phone more than books.
c) Do nothing, because the texts are about community fridges.
d) Allow phones everywhere only if users later confirm the room stayed quiet during the trial.

---

**13.** *(Alternativas — lenguaje en contexto)* In *"A trial might show us problems we cannot predict"*, what does *might* tell us about the result?

a) It expresses possibility, not certainty
b) It expresses a strong obligation
c) It expresses a habit in the past
d) It expresses a definite, proven fact

---

**14.** *(Alternativas — audiencia y propósito)* Which notice for younger students explains the librarian's trial clearly and invites feedback?

a) Phones are banned forever in the library. Please do not ask the librarian about this again.
b) Phones are allowed everywhere from today. You can play any game you like at any table.
c) The library will be closed next month for a phone trial. Please study at home instead.
d) For one month, you can use phones for research at the two tables by the door. Tell us if the room stays quiet!

---

**15.** *(Alternativas — límite de la evidencia)* The librarian has not yet tested the idea. Which question to library users would help assess the proposal?

a) "Do you prefer red or blue headphones when you study at home in the evening?"
b) "How many phones and computers does your family have at home right now?"
c) "Was the rest of the room quiet enough while people used phones at the two tables?"
d) "Which video games do you usually play at home with your friends on weekends?"

---

**16.** *(Selección múltiple)* Which concluding sentence fairly represents both texts?

a) The librarian rejects every possible use of phones, so students should stop asking for anything.
b) Both sides value research, so a short trial could balance useful phone use with a quiet space.
c) The students want to turn the quiet library into a noisy games room for everyone.

## OA N°4 — Producir textos claros para interactuar y reflexionar sobre la identidad

*(Lee el o los textos y responde las preguntas 17 a 25.)*

**Model email**

> Hi Sam,
> Our school magazine is collecting stories about objects that represent our families. I chose my grandmother's old teapot, although nobody uses it anymore. She brought it from Chiloé when she moved to the city, and it reminds me that our family story started far from here. Readers are invited to send photos of their own objects. Could you tell us about an object that matters to you?
> Best,
> Jamie

Puedes usar experiencias reales o inventadas; no incluyas datos privados ni direcciones.
