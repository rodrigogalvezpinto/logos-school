# PRUEBA DE INGLÉS — 6° Básico — FORMA B
## Examen de Práctica estilo Examen Libre de Validación de Estudios (Mineduc)

**Asignatura:** Idioma Extranjero — Inglés
**Curso:** 6° Básico
**N° de preguntas:** 25
**Tiempo:** 90 minutos
**Materiales permitidos:** Se permite el uso de **diccionario** (impreso) durante toda
la prueba. No se permite el uso de calculadora, celular ni apuntes.

### Instrucciones para el estudiante

1. Lee cada pregunta y su texto antes de responder. No necesitas memorizar las
   respuestas de la guía: aquí encontrarás situaciones nuevas sobre los mismos temas.
2. Marca una alternativa (a, b, c o d). En la pregunta de verdadero o falso, marca True
   (verdadero) o False (falso).
3. Puedes usar diccionario para buscar palabras que no conozcas.
4. Cada pregunta vale un punto. No hay descuento por respuestas incorrectas.
5. Lee todas las alternativas antes de elegir: solo una cumple exactamente lo que pide la
   consigna.
6. Administra tu tiempo: te recomendamos no más de 3 a 4 minutos por pregunta.

## OA N°5 — Habilidades, direcciones, hora y presente vs. pasado

**1.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"My grandfather is a mechanic. He can fix
cars and motorcycles, but he can't drive a bus."* What can't the grandfather do?

a) Work as a mechanic
b) Fix cars
c) Fix motorcycles
d) Drive a bus

**2.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Get out of the subway station and walk to
the traffic light. Cross the zebra crossing. The restaurant is behind the
church."* Where is the restaurant?

a) In front of the church
b) Behind the church
c) Next to the subway station
d) Between the traffic light and the church

**3.** *(Alternativas — completar)* The bus to Valparaíso leaves at 7:30.
Complete with one word: *"The bus leaves at half ___ seven."*

a) after
b) to
c) past
d) half

**4.** *(Alternativas — comprensión)* Read: *"Now Florencia takes
the subway to school. Last year she went to school by bicycle, because she
lived near the school."* *How did Florencia go to school
last year?*

a) She takes the subway to school.
b) She went to school by subway.
c) She goes to school by bicycle.
d) She went to school by bicycle.

**5.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Read: *"This is Joaquín's farm. There
are nine cows, fourteen hens and two horses."* Complete with the number in
words: *"Joaquín has ___ hens."*

a) forty
b) fourteen
c) nine
d) four

## OA N°6 — Notas, recetas, avisos y correos sobre temas cotidianos

**6.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"NEW! Happy Teeth Dental Clinic. Free
check-up for children every Saturday morning. Call 22 555 0101."* What is the
purpose of this text?

a) To tell a story about a dentist
b) To give a recipe
c) To describe the weather
d) To advertise a dental clinic

**7.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Hello Ms. Castro, Benjamín can't come to
the swimming class on Thursday. He caught a cold. He will be back next week.
Thank you, Ana (Benjamín's mother)."* When will Benjamín come back to the
swimming class?

a) On Thursday
b) Today
c) Next week
d) Tomorrow

**8.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete the dialogue at a restaurant:
*"— Can I ___ the chicken soup, please? — Here you are. — Thank you! — You're
___."*

a) has / welcome
b) have / thank you
c) have / welcome
d) eat / sorry

**9.** *(Alternativas — comprensión)* Read: *"Sandwich Corner.
Cheese sandwich: $2.500. Egg sandwich: $2.200. Orange juice: $1.200. Open from
8 am to 4 pm."* *What time does Sandwich Corner close?*

a) It closes at 4 pm.
b) It closes at 8 am.
c) It opens at 4 pm.
d) It closes at 2.500.

**10.** *(Verdadero o falso)* Read the note: *"Mom, I'm at
Grandma's house. We are having lunch together. I will be home at 6. Rocío."*
Write True or False: *Rocío is having breakfast with her grandmother.*

## OA N°8 — Reaccionar y opinar sobre lo que leo en inglés

La pregunta 12 comprueba comprensión literal (OA 5) como apoyo; las demás preguntas de este apartado evalúan la reacción a lo leído (OA 8).

**11.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"On our boat trip, a huge whale swam next
to us and jumped out of the water!"* Which sentence is a personal reaction
(feeling) to this text?

a) Whales live in the sea and eat small fish.
b) There was one big whale next to the boat.
c) The trip was on a boat, near the coast.
d) I feel excited when I read about the whale.

**12.** *(Selección múltiple)* Read: *"Isidora visited the Atacama Desert with
her class. At night she saw thousands of stars and she felt very happy."* How
did Isidora feel at night?

a) Very happy
b) Bored
c) Tired
d) Angry

**13.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete with the correct word: *"I
like this story ___ the animals in it are very funny."*

a) but
b) or
c) so
d) because

**14.** *(Alternativas — opinión con razón)* Read: *"In the story, a
small penguin gets lost and a friendly seal helps it find its family."*
Which answer to *Which character do you like more? Why?* gives a character and a reason that fits the story?

a) I like the seal more because it gets lost in the story.
b) I like the seal more because it is friendly and helps the penguin.
c) I likes the penguin because it is small and lost.
d) The seal is a story about a family and a penguin.

**15.** *(Alternativas — opinión con razón)* Read: *"Antonia traveled to
the south by train. She thinks trains are fun because you can see lakes and
forests from the window."* Which is a correct answer to *Do you like traveling by train? Why or why not?*

a) Yes, I does, because trains.
b) Antonia traveled by train.
c) Yes, I do, because I like looking out of the window.
d) No, I don't, because forests are green the window.

## OA N°14 — Escribir textos siguiendo un modelo

**16.** *(Selección múltiple)* Which word completes the invitation correctly?
*"Come to my pool party! It is ___ December 15th, from 3 to 6 pm."*

a) in
b) on
c) at
d) under

**17.** *(Selección múltiple)* Choose the word that rhymes and completes the
rhyme: *"I have a little cat, / it sleeps on a ___."*

a) mat
b) bed
c) chair
d) sofa

**18.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete the email with the greeting
and the closing: *"___ Grandpa, We are in Puerto Montt. It is cold and rainy,
but we are having fun. ___, Ignacio."*

a) Dear / See you soon
b) See you soon / Dear
c) Hello / Dear
d) Dear / Once upon a time

**19.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete the comic strip with one of
the sentences given. Panel 1, Rabbit: *"I'm so hungry!"* Panel 2, Fox: *"___"*
Panel 3, Rabbit: *"Thank you! These carrots are delicious."*

a) Here you are, some carrots!
b) It's very cold today.
c) I can't swim.

**20.** *(Alternativas — escribir siguiendo un modelo)* Which postcard completes the model correctly: *"Hi ___! I'm on vacation in ___. Every day we
travel by ___. See you soon, ___."*

a) Hi bus! I'm on vacation in Martín. Every day we travel by La Serena. See you soon, Paula.
b) Hi Martín! I'm on vacation in bus. Every day we travel by Paula. See you soon, La Serena.
c) Hi! I'm vacation La Serena bus. See you, Paula.
d) Hi Martín! I'm on vacation in La Serena. Every day we travel by bus. See you soon, Paula.

## OA N°15 — Describir, contrastar, presente continuo y preguntar

**21.** *(Selección múltiple)* Complete the dialogue: *"— Is your sister
sleeping? — No, ___. She is doing her homework."*

a) she is
b) she isn't
c) she doesn't
d) she wasn't

**22.** *(Selección múltiple)* Choose the correct word: *"Five ___ are playing
football in the park."*

a) childs
b) childrens
c) children
d) child's

**23.** *(Alternativas — completar)* Complete to express necessity: *"I ___
to wear a helmet when I ride my bike."*

a) has
b) have
c) having
d) am

**24.** *(Alternativas — completar con contraste)* Complete: *"The turtle
walks very ___, but the rabbit runs ___."*

a) fast / slowly
b) slow / fastly
c) slowly / fast
d) quickly / slowly

**25.** *(Alternativas — formular preguntas)* Which is the correct question for this answer? Use *How many*: *"There are sixteen books on the desk."*

a) How many books are there on the desk?
b) How much books are there on the desk?
c) How many books is there on the desk?
d) How many are books on the desk?
